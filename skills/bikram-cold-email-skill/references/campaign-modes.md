# Own-Product, Affiliate, and ClickBank Email Modes

Selling someone else's product is supported. Separate **who sells the offer** from **why the recipient may receive this email**. Neither an affiliate account nor MailWizz subscriber status grants permission to market.

## Choose the mode before writing

| Mode | Use | Writing and CTA | Required evidence |
|------|-----|-----------------|-------------------|
| `b2b_cold` | Relevant business outreach where applicable law and delivery-provider policy permit it | Short, honest peer-style email; usually a reply-first CTA and no marketing link in the first touch | Business relevance, lawful contact basis, verified addresses, provider acceptance, opt-out, sender identity and postal address |
| `permission_based_promotion` | An own-product or third-party offer to an audience with documented permission covering this sender and this kind of promotion | Honest promotional subject, a clear benefit and one CTA; an approved offer link can appear in email one | Consent/permission source, scope and date, expected frequency, offer terms, unsubscribe and suppression, provider permission |

Own-product, affiliate, and ClickBank relationships can apply within these modes only when the applicable program and sending rules allow it. Do not automatically convert a purchased or scraped list to permission-based promotion. If permission or provider acceptance is unknown, create a clearly blocked local draft only; do not schedule. This skill does not add general lifecycle/nurture automation or non-email outreach.

## Own-product offers

Use the client's approved product identity, proof, positioning, and terms. Apply the B2B cold references only in `b2b_cold` mode. For a permission-based product promotion, use the consent scope and offer-specific CTA rather than forcing an interest-reply or meeting request. Never invent case studies or treat framework examples as client evidence.

## Affiliate offers

- Record the actual seller, product, program reference, permitted email methods, approved URL, prohibited claims, and disclosure requirements on the offer. Do not write "we built" or "our product" when the client is a promoter rather than the seller.
- Require a clear, conspicuous affiliate disclosure near the recommendation/CTA, for example: "I may earn a commission if you buy through this link." Use the operator's approved accurate wording and applicable local requirements; do not hide it in an unrelated footer or invent a no-extra-cost promise.
- Use the exact operator-approved HTTPS affiliate URL, including attribution parameters. Do not create affiliate IDs, edit HopLink attribution, shorten or cloak links, or add tracking redirects without approval. If click tracking is explicitly selected, confirm supported handling preserves the approved destination and attribution; do not silently rewrite the link. Tracking defaults off; preserving affiliate attribution is different from enabling MailWizz click tracking.
- Check the program's current email, subject-line, trademark, brand-bidding, territory, and disclosure restrictions. A sales swipe is inspiration, not substantiation or permission. Preserve required notices even when shortening the email.
- Do not imply a personal purchase, product use, neutral review, expert endorsement, or exclusive deal without evidence. No fabricated testimonials, medical cures, guaranteed income, fake discounts, or resetting countdowns.

## ClickBank promotions

Record `commercial_relationship: clickbank_affiliate`, the actual seller and offer, the approved HopLink or vendor-authorized destination, affiliate program instructions, and current terms. ClickBank is a marketplace, not proof that a claim or audience is approved.

- Review the seller's affiliate rules and ClickBank's current [Promotional Guidelines](https://support.clickbank.com/en/articles/10535354-promotional-guidelines) and [Advertising Guidelines](https://support.clickbank.com/en/articles/10535342-advertising-guidelines) before a new offer. Store review evidence; re-check when terms or copy materially change.
- Confirm whether this seller/program and sending provider allow the proposed email method. Do not assert a blanket ClickBank opt-in-only rule or blanket cold-email permission: eligibility depends on program terms, provider policy, audience, and law. When any prohibits the campaign, do not schedule it.
- ClickBank's email rules prohibit misleading headers/subjects and require applicable opt-out and advertisement identification. Keep both that identification and the affiliate disclosure where required. Do not disguise a promotion as an existing conversation, an account warning, or personal correspondence.
- Verify price, recurring billing, actual deadlines, refunds/guarantees, and approved claims against current seller material. Do not promise terms that are absent or expired. Explain recurring billing when price is mentioned, not just the introductory amount.
- Health, supplements, weight-loss, investment, and income offers require substantiated claims and human compliance review. Do not infer a recipient's diagnosis, finances, or other sensitive attributes from targeting. Disclaimers cannot rescue an unsupported performance claim.
- For time-bound promotions, the entire sending window, including throttled or queued delivery, must end before the verified deadline in the specified time zone. Verify a provider-enforced cutoff or an authorized stop-and-queue reconciliation that prevents post-deadline sends; a start time before expiry is insufficient. If this cannot be verified, block the schedule or remove the time-bound offer in a newly approved version. If the product becomes unavailable or terms change after approval, stop pending sends and obtain revised approval. Do not guarantee the time a recipient's mail server delivers or displays the message.

## Promotional output and sequence rules

- Match supplied subjects and swipes to documented claims; flag deceptive or unsupported elements instead of copying them. Preserve requested wording only when safe and accurate.
- One primary CTA can link to the approved sales/affiliate destination in the first promotional email. B2B cold first touches often work as reply-first notes, but an operator-requested approved website in the signature is allowed; do not impose a blanket first-touch link ban. Links never waive disclosure, permission or campaign approval.
- Do not apply B2B cold reply-rate benchmarks as expected promotional conversion results. Use verified client data; report revenue or purchases only when actual attribution evidence is available.
- Promotional sequences need approved frequency, permission scope, deadline, and stop rules. Unsubscribes, complaints, and hard bounces always block further sends within the applicable scope. A conversational reply pauses automated follow-ups for review. Purchase-based stopping requires a verified purchase source and matching; if unavailable, do not promise it or schedule a sequence whose eligibility depends on it.
- Use the same versioned approval packet and MailWizz readback as other campaigns. Each variant or step scheduled must be explicitly included. Operator approval cannot authorize unlawful or program-prohibited promotion.
