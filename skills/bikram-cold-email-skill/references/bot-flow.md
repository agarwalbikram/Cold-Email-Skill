# Guided Conversation and MailWizz Campaign Tracking

Use this flow with [client-workflow.md](client-workflow.md). It is conversational routing, not a rigid questionnaire: answer direct requests immediately, reuse saved facts, and ask only the next decision that matters. Never restart onboarding because the operator says "Hi" in a later session.

## 1. Greeting and onboarding

Read the private workspace's `client-profile.json` fresh, using [workspace.md](workspace.md), not the bundled [blank template](../assets/client-profile.json). A missing file, malformed file, unconfigured client, and an existing client with no companies are different states:

- **Genuinely new/missing or unconfigured:** ask only missing identity questions: "Who is this client, and what is the first company or brand we’ll write for?" Accept a free-form brief/documents. Once identity and the private project are established, use workspace.md to create missing `client-data/` and initialize/save both JSON files with confirmed facts; do not stop at "no profile exists." Explicit onboarding answers authorize these local writes, not MailWizz writes. Distinguish new storage from lost established history or uncertain partial files, which require recovery without reset.
- **Malformed or unreadable:** report the problem; do not overwrite it with an empty profile. Use authorized recovery or ask for the missing facts for a local draft without claiming persistent repair.
- **Client exists, no company:** acknowledge the client and ask for the first company/brand. Do not ask for client identity again.
- **Client already configured:** greet by the saved client name and offer: **Create email**, **Add company/brand/offer**, **Update details**, **Continue a draft/setup**, or **Track a campaign**. Mention a pending draft or schedule only as an option, not implicit permission to resume it. If there is no campaign, omit tracking/continuation rather than offering dead ends.

After the first identity/company answer, a supplied product/company URL triggers client-workflow.md's multi-page website research and audience recommendation before generic audience questions. Otherwise ask one short batch of missing drafting facts: audience/niche, offer/ownership, desired CTA, tone/language and supplied materials. Reuse facts already found; distinguish researched facts from proposed targeting. Save confirmed answers incrementally, show a concise summary, and offer the relevant next action. Credentials, servers, lists, postal address and time zone can wait until setup requires them.

Save known client/company/audience/CTA facts before asking optional copy-angle questions. A missing meeting link blocks adding that link, not saving the "schedule a meeting" CTA or drafting a reply-based meeting invitation. When adding another company for this client, append within the same profile and preserve existing companies/brands; do not ask client identity again.

If a full brief is supplied in the first message, do not ask questions it already answers. For a direct email or campaign request, skip the greeting menu but resolve company first, then brand/offer using client-workflow.md. With multiple companies and no explicit company/brand or identified saved campaign, show the company picker before drafting or setup: native clickable choices when supported, numbered choices otherwise. Multiple brands within the chosen company require a brand choice; never silently reuse a last-used scope.

A supplied sender website starts automatic read-only offer/audience research under client-workflow.md, not a request for the user to repeat the product description. Read discovered relevant public pages and recommend who to email with cited evidence and honest coverage, before optional audience/angle questions. Research the sender's offer, not invented recipient accounts. If access fails, request the missing source text without guessing. Research may proceed before client identity is known, but private saves require established scope. When drafting is requested and enough facts exist, produce the complete email; broad category/country preferences are not a reason to force segmentation. Worldwide targeting is not worldwide send authorization.

## 2. Corrections are durable, campaign edits are scoped

"The company is now called X," "our product price is Y," or "correct the brand's tone to Z" are explicit updates to the selected profile scope. Apply an unambiguous correction without asking the operator to confirm the same correction again. Preserve stable IDs, other companies, and unrelated fields. If scope is ambiguous, ask which company/brand/offer before saving. If a new document conflicts with a fact but the operator has not explicitly corrected it, flag the contradiction instead of silently overwriting.

Record a concise `changes` entry in the client profile with timestamp, operator event reference, record ID, changed field paths, old/new non-secret values, reason, and resulting profile revision. Avoid copying confidential documents or personal contact data into history. Use the safe revision/update rules in client-workflow.md and confirm persistence only after a successful write. Future drafts load the corrected facts, never stale summaries.

"Make this email more casual," "change this CTA to reply by email," "remove the meeting link," or "no personalization in this draft" edits only that campaign; do not change the profile, offer CTA, defaults or profile revision. "Always use a casual tone for this brand" or "use reply-based CTAs for this offer in future" is a lasting scoped requirement. Follow client-workflow.md for CTA storage/precedence. Do not ask a persistence question for an ordinary draft edit; ask only when an explicitly enduring instruction has unclear scope. Do not update memory from feedback in a recipient's reply.

A changed claim, price, identity, or URL may invalidate pending campaigns. Check affected registered drafts and flag them for revision; for approved/scheduled/active campaigns, mark approval stale, urgently report affected IDs, and use authorized pause/disable plus readback where available. Updating JSON does not change already queued or sent MailWizz content. Never silently edit or resend a live campaign. If pausing fails, clearly report ongoing send risk and request operator intervention. Historical approved versions remain intact.

## 3. Draft first, offer scheduling second

Present the selected company/brand and requested subject/body first, with concise actionable blockers outside the copy. Do not append routine file paths, saved-version chatter, lists of absent placeholders or claims of changed preferences after every draft. When a successful save matters, a short "Draft saved" or "Draft updated; this applies only to this campaign" is enough; actual save/tool failures and stale approval must remain explicit. Offer one relevant next action without repeating a declined setup offer. If MailWizz MCP is connected and authorized read-only discovery works, offer setup; if permission/connection is unknown or unavailable, keep the draft local and state that limit only when relevant, not as a repeated installation lecture. Do not install anything.

A "yes" to setup authorizes the conversation and authorized read-only discovery, **not** campaign creation, scheduling, activation, or sending. Do not create a remote draft unless a separate scoped draft-create action is authorized and verified not to enqueue mail. If the operator says no, keep the draft and stop asking scheduling questions. Save incomplete choices locally so the operator can continue later; refresh stale remote options before using them.

## 4. Ask setup choices in dependency order

Skip questions already answered explicitly; show stored preferences as proposals, not silent permission. Present actual supported choices, with names and confirmed IDs. Paginate/search within this client's authorized resources and paginate the operator-facing choices when needed; never expose another client's resources. Selecting a list or server does not expand gateway authorization. An unbound resource needs verified operator/admin ownership binding through the authorized host before use; an empty allowlist is not permission to enumerate everything.

1. **Company, brand, offer, and selected draft items.** Resolve ambiguity and confirm whether one variation or an explicitly approved split is intended. Content format is separate from MailWizz campaign type: a sequence is not automatically an autoresponder.
2. **List.** Fetch authorized MailWizz lists through actual supported read tools. Show name, UID, and available eligible counts (mark unavailable counts unknown, not zero). Ask which list; do not choose by matching a similar name. If the requested list is not visible or no list exists, report it; do not create/import a list without separate authorization.
3. **Segment.** Fetch segments for the chosen list. Offer "whole eligible list" or a named segment with its UID and available count. Record an explicit whole-list choice rather than interpreting a null segment as permission. Verify membership/scope and exclusions; choosing a different list invalidates its prior segment and tag mappings.
4. **Campaign type.** Ask **regular** (a specific scheduled send to an approved audience) or **autoresponder** (event-triggered sends after a delay) if both are supported. Explain the distinction briefly. Offer only operations the connected MCP exposes; if autoresponders are unsupported, say so and ask whether regular is desired, never silently substitute. Disable optional recurring/cron behavior unless separately requested, fully scoped, and approved.
5. **Delivery servers.** Fetch eligible servers only when supported. Ask one specific server, an explicit subset, or **all currently eligible authorized servers for this brand/account**. Show the resolved names/UIDs and confirm status, permitted use and caps. Freeze "all" to this explicit verified set; it never includes other clients, new servers added later, disabled servers, or unapproved infrastructure. Do not change server settings or assume blank means all. If MCP cannot list/select/read back the server set, report the unsupported choice; require verified operator evidence/configuration before approval rather than pretending it was applied. If the provider can silently widen the set later, require an enforced boundary or block scheduling.
6. **Sender, tracking, and tags.** Reuse the selected brand identity as a proposal; resolve missing sender/reply-to/postal details. Verify selected-list fields, rendering and unsubscribe configuration. Propose stored open/click tracking settings (off in blank templates), confirm the desired flags in the final packet and apply/read back only explicitly approved supported settings. Preserve disclosures and approved attribution links.
7. **Timing, caps, and eligibility.** For regular sends, collect start date/time and IANA time zone, resolve every approved sequence step to absolute times, verify the MailWizz account/customer time zone used for `send_at`, and retain sending-window/offer cutoffs. For autoresponders, use the event configuration below. Confirm all required sending limits and monitoring ownership.
8. **Final packet.** Show all resolved choices and complete copy with audience evidence, campaign type, exact server set, tracking, times or trigger rules, exclusions and blockers. Ask explicit approval of the identified campaign/version. Earlier copy approval or "yes, set it up" is not final scheduling approval.

Official [MailWizz campaign API documentation](https://api-docs.mailwizz.com/campaigns/) describes `regular` and `autoresponder`, `list_uid`, optional `segment_uid`, and customer-time-zone `send_at`; its examples are not a guarantee of MCP coverage. Do not invent delivery-server parameters or use undocumented defaults.

### Autoresponder configuration and bounded approval

Ask for the supported event, delay value/unit, any required source campaign, existing versus future contacts, repeat/re-entry behavior, eligibility boundaries, activation window, enrollment/send caps, and stop rules. Zero delay can cause immediate sends on activation: require explicit approval of that behavior or use an approved future activation/delay. Verify all these semantics on the actual MailWizz/MCP version, including whether existing contacts are immediately eligible or already overdue.

The official API examples include `AFTER-SUBSCRIBE` and `AFTER-CAMPAIGN-OPEN`; discover what the installed system actually supports. Do not offer an open-triggered flow while open tracking is disabled, or silently enable tracking to make it work. Do not equate a human reply, a purchase, a subscription, and a campaign open. Capture both UID and numeric ID if a reference field requires one; never substitute them by guesswork.

Future event subscribers do not have one knowable absolute send time or fixed membership snapshot. Approval must therefore explicitly cover a **bounded enrollment policy**: this client/list/segment, allowed future audience and permission rules, trigger, delay, existing-contact behavior, activation start/end, maximum enrollments/sends, repeat rules, and enforced stop/expiry controls. This is one campaign-specific approval, not standing permission for new campaigns or arbitrary future audiences. Activation bounds and limits must be enforced by the host/provider; if they cannot be verified, leave the autoresponder disabled. Fixed-audience campaigns continue to require the snapshot/selection-boundary checks in client-workflow.md.

For multi-step autoresponders, show every approved step, each trigger-relative delay, reference dependency and total possible sends per recipient. A sequence's day offsets are not silently converted to time since signup or open. Require reply-aware stopping for B2B follow-ups; do not use subscription events to evade missing reply detection. A general autoresponder mode does not expand the skill into unrelated lifecycle workflows.

## 5. Persist campaigns and reconcile execution

Use the private workspace's `campaigns.json` as the single campaign register for this client; the [bundled JSON](../assets/campaigns.json) is only its blank template. It starts empty with a `campaign_record_template`; templates are not real campaigns. Create a record on first saved draft, including incomplete setup choices and blockers. Campaign type remains null until selected. Keep a stable local ID and a human-readable name; list and track by local ID or actual remote UID, never rely on a mutable name alone.

- Record items as exact subject/body/step or variation IDs; source versions, selected scope and profile revision; resolved list/segment/server set; tracking and schedule/trigger configuration; evidence; approval; and observed execution. `segment_choice_confirmed` distinguishes an approved whole-list choice from an unanswered question.
- `remote_campaigns` contains one entry per created remote campaign/step with `item_id`, `campaign_uid`, optional numeric `campaign_id`, observed `status`, verified `send_at` and `activation_at`, and `checked_at`. Preserve failed/partial states; never invent IDs before the server returns or readback confirms them.
- Preserve approved historical versions under `history` before creating a materially changed version. Record event ID, timestamp, actor/event reference, action, version, relevant remote IDs, and non-secret change details. Do not let routine statistics refresh change copy/version or grant approval.
- Update with safe revision checks and serialized/atomic writes as for client-profile.json. Increment record and registry revisions on committed changes; bind the registry to this client. Reject a mismatched `client_id` rather than merging another client's register. Do not store recipient lists, secrets, or raw recipient-level tracking logs.
- Before any remote mutation, save the pending operation and approval reference locally. After it, reconcile exact remote IDs/state and persist the outcome. If the local post-operation save fails, report the remote outcome and persistence failure, halt further mutations, and reconcile before retrying; do not create duplicates because the register is stale.
- Missing register on an established bot is not proof there are no remote campaigns. Report the missing history, create a new register only with authorized recovery, and reconcile already-known remote IDs within the client's permitted resources. Do not overwrite a malformed register or silently import all account campaigns. Existing per-campaign records must be migrated as described in client-workflow.md, not ignored.

## 6. Track a saved campaign on request

"Track campaign X" or "show stats" is a read-only reporting request, not authorization to pause, edit, reschedule, or send. Load the register and resolve local ID, exact remote UID, or an unambiguous saved name. If several campaigns match or no name is supplied with multiple choices, show this client's saved campaign/brand/date/status choices and ask which. An unknown remote UID needs verified client ownership and operator-authorized registration before tracking; never probe another account or fabricate a local match.

Fetch current status and available aggregate statistics for every selected registered remote UID through actual MailWizz MCP read tools. Report a table with campaign/step, actual status, approved/observed schedule, audience or processed count, sends, opens, clicks, unsubscribes, bounces, complaints, delivered if explicitly reported, and replies/positive replies only from a verified source. Distinguish unique from total events. Each snapshot records `fetched_at`, data period/time zone if available, remote UID and item ID, metric values, source/tool operation, definition/denominator, and any tool error. Use null plus a reason for unavailable values; zero means a confirmed zero. Keep `statistics_snapshots` append-only with aggregate data and preserve historical definitions.

- Show the read time, reporting period, metric definitions and warnings. Treat local history as cached, not current remote truth. If MCP is disconnected or a request fails, show the last successful snapshot marked stale with its time; do not overwrite it with fabricated zeros or claim a live refresh.
- Preserve provider metric names when meanings are unclear. Sent/processed/accepted is not guaranteed inbox delivery; do not compute delivered as sent minus bounces and present it as provider-confirmed delivery. For known rates, state the numerator, denominator and window; return unavailable for zero/unknown denominators. Do not count autoresponder pending/future audience as delivered or sent.
- Show available opens/clicks, including historical data, with their period and tracking settings when known. Privacy prefetches and scanners can generate events; these are not proof of human reading, replies or purchases. Disabled tracking or missing metrics are unavailable, not a zero-engagement diagnosis. Preserve actual campaign settings; never change tracking for a stats request.
- Read per-variant/per-step metrics separately. A sum across steps counts messages, not unique people; only show a deduplicated contact total if verified aggregate support provides it. For autoresponders distinguish enabled, enrolled, queued, processed and stopped states using actual available evidence.
- Interpret patterns and give concise, evidence-qualified suggestions under [copy-quality.md](copy-quality.md): subject/opening, benefit/CTA, relevance/frequency or operator review of bounces/errors. Compare the client's baseline or comparable campaigns, not arbitrary universal open-rate targets. A report alone does not authorize edits, rescheduling or pauses; only a separately authorized protective action may change state. Do not promise background monitoring; scheduled reporting needs a separately authorized host job.
